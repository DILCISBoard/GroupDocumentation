3. Common questions
    1. **Major revision planning:** this will take time, 3 years. A critical question – which version of METS will be used? METS 2.0 was released a couple of years ago, but not everyone will use it and will keep on with METS 1.0. For this major revision we can stay on METS 1.0, but for the next major revision we can cover both 1.0 and 2.0 in parallel. MF: E-ARK v2.3 has been ready for a year – can we release it now? KB: This could be done over the summer, together with the next release of the CITS 3D. We could release v2.4 and 2.5 before v3.0. We will need to discuss the AIP specification: Sven wants to keep the AIP. It is easier however to have the CSIP with the possibility of extensions to cover SIP/AIP and DIP. MF: Are there new requirements – do we have to wait for the CEN committee? JA: I suggest we do what we feel is right for us with the specifications, then worry about working with CEN when we are happy. MF: There are no new requirements for SIP – it is working perfectly. KB: This is the issue that we must decide upon before the Dublin meeting in November – we could decide to keep our current version and not make a version change. Stability is very important and can take 10 years. SIARD may or may not be changing alongside the IPs etc. – it can follow its own cycle. ABN will be convening a SIARD meeting in a week or two, and there is a new SFA employee Dominique (?) to work on SIARD. AKG: it’s best to continue with METS 1.0. KB: some users are using METS 2.0. GZ: a revision should follow public demand, so if there is none, we should leave as is. Do we have a document/map enumerating the issues from DG, but does he understand our system? MF: DG wants precise text, not e.g. general “metadata”. GZ: we cover this in the CITS – here we have the precise metadata described. KB: we will minimise the references to OAIS in the specifications, and address the issue fully in the guidelines. Clearly E-ARK is having an impact, e.g. it is being used in Wellington NZ, which we didn’t know about. (The ‘E-ARK in the wild” EU survey is ready to go, and could be sent out beginning of June until the end of June. (FYI new EU surveys will not be available to non-EU institutions after August)). SS: the only topic for the AIP is OCFL. We will need to decide if we want to just have one specification with three extensions (major revision) or not. Sven will tell us his thoughts for AIP next time.
